Tapsilog Recipe

Tapsilog is a popular Filipino breakfast meal made with tapa (marinated beef), sinangag (garlic fried rice), and itlog (fried egg). It is a simple yet flavorful dish commonly served with vinegar, atchara, or other condiments.

Ingredients:

  • Beef: 250g thinly sliced beef sirloin or flank steak
  • Tapa Marinade: 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp vinegar, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 4 minced garlic cloves, a pinch of salt and pepper
  • Sinangag (Garlic Rice): 2 cups leftover cold rice, 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp oil, salt
  • Itlog (Egg): 1 egg
  • Cooking oil: For frying

Cooking Procedure:

How to Make Beef Tapa:

  • Mix soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, garlic, pepper, and salt in a bowl.
  • Add the beef slices and mix well.
  • Rest the beef in the fridge for 2 hours or overnight.
  • Put the beef and its marinade in a pan.
  • Cook on medium heat until the liquid is gone.
  • Add a little oil and fry the beef until it turns brown and slightly sweet.

How to Make Beef Sinangag (Garlic Rice):

  • Heat oil in a pan on medium heat.
  • Add minced garlic and cook until light brown and fragrant.
  • Add the cold rice and break up any hard lumps.
  • Stir well for 3 to 5 minutes.
  • Add salt to taste.

How to Finish and Serve

  • HFry the egg in a separate pan with oil until the edges are crispy and the yolk is soft.
  • Put the garlic rice, beef tapa, and fried egg on a plate.
  • Add sliced fresh tomatoes and a small bowl of spiced vinegar for dipping.
  • SERVE WITH A SMILE

For presentation, place the garlic rice on one side, tapa in the center, and the fried egg beside the rice. Garnish with sliced tomatoes or spring onions for a fresh touch.
